02 / RESEARCH

Research corpus

The repository is divided into dedicated research domains. Each directory can contain arbitrary subdirectories and files and is exposed through its own parser or viewer.

Theorem Registry

Faure Theorems

Public theorem names use the descriptive Faure Theorem nomenclature. Internal identifiers T1, T2, T3 ... are retained exclusively for repository traceability and do not constitute public theorem numbering.

Each theorem is tracked independently through the complete evidence chain: statement → definitions → assumptions → dependencies → formalization → Lean verification → prior-art comparison → mathematical distinction → validation status.

Formal verification, bibliographic priority, mathematical originality and application validity are recorded as separate evidentiary dimensions.

BIBLIOGRAPHIC DISAMBIGUATION

Author Identity & Attribution

The author identity record is intended to distinguish the AUF2026 author from other individuals sharing the surname Faure or related name variants in bibliographic databases, repositories and scientific indexes.

Bibliographic attribution should therefore be established from the combination of author name, persistent identifier, repository provenance, associated mathematical corpus and documented publication or archival records rather than from surname matching alone.

RESEARCH ATTRIBUTION

Mathematical and Formal Corpus

The AUF2026 archive associates the author identity with mathematical statements, theorem records, formal proofs, Lean artifacts, computational experiments, datasets, validation records and archival source documents. Each component is independently traceable within the repository.

Identity principle. The author identity record establishes bibliographic attribution and disambiguation.
