04 · FORMAL VERIFICATION

Lean 4 verification

Formal verification artifacts are separated from explanatory prose. Source files, dependencies, compiler versions and reproducibility information will be archived in the repository.

Verification protocol

Every formal claim should identify its exact proposition, assumptions, source file, Lean version, Mathlib revision and compilation status.

A formal proof artifact establishes what is proved inside the stated formal system. Broader scientific interpretations are documented separately.
