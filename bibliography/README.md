AUF2026 — Bibliography

This directory contains the bibliographic record and comparative literature analysis.

Objectives

The bibliography distinguishes:

prior mathematical results;

independent rediscoveries;

definitions already present in the literature;

formally verified results;

computational methods;

claimed extensions;

unresolved questions.

Comparative protocol

For each relevant source:

full citation;

DOI or persistent identifier where available;

source URL;

publication date;

mathematical object;

theorem/result;

method;

relationship to AUF2026 material;

exact overlap;

claimed distinction;

verification status.

Comparisons are documented at the level of individual mathematical statements rather than broad thematic similarity.
